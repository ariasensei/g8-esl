Reading activity · 10 minutes

Read: Which Hobby is Better?

Which Hobby is Better?

Read, then answer!
📚Emma

Emma enjoys reading books. In her opinion, reading is exciting because she can learn new ideas and visit different worlds in her imagination. She thinks reading helps people become more creative.

🎮Lucas

Lucas enjoys playing video games. He believes games are fun because he can solve problems and play with friends. He agrees that reading is useful, but he prefers video games after school.

Emma and Lucas have different opinions, but they listen respectfully. They do not argue.

Writing activity · 12 minutes

My Opinion

Writing task

Write a short paragraph about the best hobby for teenagers. Use at least one opinion phrase and two reasons.

  • 1.What hobby do you choose?
  • 2.Why is it good?
  • 3.What skills can people learn?
  • 4.Why do you recommend it?
  • 5.How does it make people feel?

Example paragraph

In my opinion, reading is the best hobby for teenagers. I think it is interesting because books can take us to different worlds. Reading helps people learn new words and become more creative. I recommend reading because it is relaxing and useful. It can make people feel calm, curious, and happy.
