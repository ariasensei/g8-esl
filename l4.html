Grammar studio

Will for the Future

Use will with a base verb for predictions, decisions made now, offers, and promises.

Positive

Subject + will + base verb

I will study tonight. She will travel next year. We will watch a movie.

Negative

Subject + will not (won’t) + base verb

I won’t be late. He won’t forget. They won’t miss the bus.

Questions

Will + subject + base verb?

Will you come tomorrow? Will she pass the exam? Will they play basketball?

Predictions

I think it will rain. People will live longer. Cars will become smarter.

Instant Decisions

“I’m thirsty.” → “I will get you some water.”

Offers

I’ll help you. I’ll carry your bag. I’ll open the door.

Promises

I will study harder. I won’t forget your birthday. I will do my homework.

Will or Be Going To?

Will = prediction / instant decision / promise / offer Be going to = planned action / already decided Will: “I think it will rain.” Be going to: “I’m going to bring an umbrella.”

Choose the use of will to see an example

Prediction: I think people will live on Mars one day.

Decision: The phone is ringing. I will answer it.

Offer: Your books look heavy. I will carry them.

Promise: I will always try my best.

Reading activity

The World in 2050

Read and notice the future predictions

Many people believe life will be different in the future. Cars will drive themselves, and robots will help with everyday chores. Schools will use more virtual reality, allowing students to explore history and science in exciting ways. Doctors will use advanced technology to treat diseases more effectively. However, people will still need kindness, friendship, and creativity because these qualities cannot be replaced by machines.
