Reading activity

Three Amazing Cities

Read, compare, and notice the adjectives

Tokyo is bigger and more crowded than Paris. It has more people, brighter lights, and faster trains. Paris is smaller, but many visitors think it is more romantic and more beautiful. Sydney is one of the most comfortable cities for outdoor life because it has warm weather, beaches, and open spaces. All three cities are interesting, but each city is special in a different way.

Writing activity

My Favorite Place

Write a short paragraph about a place you love. Use at least two comparatives and two superlatives.

Guide questions

What is your favorite place?

What is it more beautiful, quieter, or cleaner than?

What is the best thing about this place?

Why is it the most special place for you?

My paragraph planner

Sample paragraph

My favorite place is Baguio City. It is cooler and quieter than Manila. The parks are more relaxing, and the air feels fresher. For me, Baguio has the most beautiful views in the Philippines. It is one of the most special places because I can spend time with my family there.
