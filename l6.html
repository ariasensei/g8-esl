10 minutes · Build a strong answer

Grammar Studio: O-R-E

Good opinions do not stop after one sentence. Use O-R-E: Opinion → Reason → Example.

O · Opinion

Say clearly what you think: “I think that...” “I believe that...” “In my opinion,...” “I feel that...” “From my point of view,...”

R · Reason

Explain why. Use because to connect your opinion and reason.

E · Example

Make your reason clearer: “For example,...” “For instance,...” “One example is...”

Useful expressions

Opinion: I think that... · I believe that... · In my opinion,... · I feel that... · From my point of view,...

Reason: because

Example: For example,... · For instance,... · One example is...

However introduces another side. Japanese: しかし / しかしながら · Simplified Chinese: 然而

Model answer

In my opinion, students should have less homework because they need enough time to rest. For example, students may already spend seven hours at school before completing assignments at home.

10 minutes · Timed reading

Timed Reading: Homework and Balance

Start the timer, read closely, then rate how the passage felt for you.

READING TIME

00:00

Homework has been part of school life for many years. Teachers often give homework because it allows students to practice what they learned in class. It can also help students develop responsibility and better study habits. However, some students believe that they receive too much homework. After spending many hours at school, students may still need to complete several assignments at home. This can reduce the amount of time they have for exercise, hobbies, family, and sleep. Researchers and teachers continue to discuss how much homework students actually need. Homework can be effective when it has a clear purpose and helps students review important skills. However, giving students a large amount of work does not always mean that they will learn more. Students also need balance. Rest and free time can help them concentrate better when they return to their studies. For this reason, some people believe schools should focus on the quality of homework rather than the quantity. In my opinion, homework should not completely disappear. It can be useful, but teachers should make sure that assignments are meaningful and manageable.

Teacher Notes

Focus on Extending Answers

The main focus is extending answers rather than perfect grammar. Encourage learners to communicate a clear idea, then add a reason and an example.

Useful follow-ups

Ask: “Why?” “Can you give me an example?” “What would someone who disagrees with you say?”

Growth example

From: “I think homework is bad.” To: “I think students should have less homework because they need time to rest. For example, students can exercise or sleep enough after school.”
