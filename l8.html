Reading Practice

Online Learning vs. Classroom Learning

Online learning and classroom learning are both common ways for students to study. They have the same goal: helping students gain knowledge and develop skills. However, the learning experience can be very different.

One advantage of online learning is flexibility. Students can often study from home and use digital resources whenever they need them. It is also convenient for students who live far from school. However, online learning may require strong self-discipline because students must manage their own time.

Classroom learning, on the other hand, gives students more face-to-face interaction. They can ask questions directly, work with classmates, and participate in activities in the same room. Whereas online learning depends heavily on technology, classroom learning often depends more on direct communication.

Both forms of learning can be effective. The best choice may depend on a student's learning style, schedule, and personal needs.

Grammar Focus: Comparatives and Contrast Words

Comparatives

Use -er or more to compare two things.

small → smaller

easy → easier

interesting → more interesting

Contrast Words

while / whereas connect two different ideas.

however starts a contrasting sentence.

Example: E-books are lighter than printed books, whereas printed books do not need batteries.

Practice

1. A laptop is usually ______ than a desktop computer.

2. Classroom learning offers more face-to-face interaction, ______ online learning offers more flexibility.

3. Online learning is convenient. ______, some students may feel less connected.
