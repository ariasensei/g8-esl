Grammar studio

Will vs. Be going to

Both forms talk about the future, but they have different jobs.

Will

Prediction / quick decision

  • I will travel next year.
  • Maybe it will rain tomorrow.
  • I’m thirsty. I will get some water.

Be going to

Already planned

  • I am going to study tonight.
  • She is going to visit her grandmother.
  • We are going to watch a movie.

Simple rule

Will = not planned / prediction

Going to = planned before speaking

Guided practice

Check Your Future Grammar

Choose the best answer for each sentence, then check your answers.

1. I __ (will / am going to) do my homework tonight.

Correct answer: am going to — it is a planned action tonight.

2. It __ (will / is going to) be sunny tomorrow, I think.

Correct answer: will — this is a prediction.

3. We __ (will / are going to) visit the zoo on Saturday.

Correct answer: are going to — Saturday zoo visit is planned.

4. I forgot my pen. I __ (will / am going to) buy one now.

Correct answer: will — buying a pen is a quick decision now.

5. My family __ (will / is going to) travel during the vacation.

Correct answer: is going to — family travel is already planned.
