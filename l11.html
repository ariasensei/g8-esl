3Timed Reading: One Small Choice

Read aloud clearly. Target: about 1 minute 30 seconds–2 minutes. (10 minutes)

One Small Choice

On Monday morning, Mina woke up late. She had not prepared her schoolbag the night before, so she spent ten minutes looking for her notebook. By the time she left home, the bus had already gone. Mina arrived at school after the first lesson started.

That evening, Mina decided to change her routine. “If I prepare my bag before bed, I will save time in the morning,” she thought. She also set two alarms and put her phone away earlier than usual.

On Tuesday, Mina woke up on time. She had everything she needed, and she caught the bus easily. Her friend asked how she had changed so quickly. Mina smiled and said, “If you make small, responsible decisions, you can avoid big problems.”

Mina knew that not every day would be perfect. However, she understood that planning ahead could give her a better chance of success.

4Grammar: The First Conditional

Use the first conditional to talk about real future possibilities. (10 minutes)

Rule

If + present simple, will + base verb

If I practice, I will improve.

Reverse the order

Will + base verb + if + present simple

I will improve if I practice.

Important: Do not use will immediately after if in a standard first conditional: ❌ If I will study... → ✅ If I study... . Use a comma when the if clause comes first.

Unless = If Not

You will miss the bus unless you leave now. = You will miss the bus if you do not leave now.

8Homework: My Better Week

Write 8–10 sentences

Prompt: What three changes will you make next week to improve your routine?

✓ Write a clear goal.
✓ Use if at least twice.
✓ Use unless once.
✓ Explain at least two results.
✓ Give a realistic example.
✓ End with a conclusion.

Writing guide: Goal → If-condition → Expected result → Another condition → Example → Conclusion
