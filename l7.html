Key Idea

Opinion

What you think or believe.

Reason

Why you think that way.

Example

Information that supports your reason.

Example: I think students should exercise every day because exercise helps them stay healthy. For example, walking or playing sports can strengthen the body.

Reading Practice

Should Teenagers Have Less Homework?

Many teenagers spend several hours doing homework after school. Some people believe that homework is necessary because it gives students additional practice. However, others believe that students receive too much homework.

In my opinion, teenagers should have less homework. First, students need enough time to rest after spending many hours at school. A tired student may find it difficult to concentrate and learn effectively.

Second, teenagers need time for other important activities. They may exercise, spend time with their families, develop hobbies, or participate in clubs and sports. These activities can also teach valuable skills.

Homework is still important, but the amount should be reasonable. Students need practice, but they also need a healthy balance between schoolwork and their personal lives.

Grammar Focus: Because, Since, and So

BECAUSE

Result + because + reason

I prefer studying at home because it is quiet.

SINCE

Since + reason, result

Since I can concentrate better at home, I prefer studying there.

SO

Reason + so + result

My house is quiet, so I can concentrate better.

Easy rule: BECAUSE = result + why.    SO = reason + what happened.

Practice

2. I have an exam tomorrow, ______ I need to study tonight.
