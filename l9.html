Reading Practice

The Problem of Too Much Screen Time

Many teenagers use phones, tablets, computers, and game consoles every day. Technology can be useful for learning and communication, but too much screen time may create problems.

One cause of excessive screen time is easy access to entertainment. Social media, games, and videos are available at any time. As a result, some students stay online longer than they planned. This can reduce the amount of time they spend studying, exercising, or sleeping.

Another effect is tiredness. Students who use devices late at night may sleep less. Because they are tired the next day, they may find it difficult to focus in class.

There are several possible solutions. Students can set daily screen-time limits, turn off unnecessary notifications, and keep devices away during study time. Families can also create technology-free times, such as during meals or before bedtime. These small changes can help students use technology in a healthier way.

Grammar Focus: Cause-and-Effect Connectors

because

Shows the cause.

I was tired because I slept late.

so

Shows the result.

I slept late, so I was tired.

as a result

Introduces an effect.

I missed the bus. As a result, I was late.

Remember: Cause = why it happened. Effect = what happened next.

Practice

1. I forgot to set my alarm, ______ I woke up late.

2. I was late ______ I woke up late.

3. The internet stopped working. ______, the online class was delayed.

Homework

Write 8–10 sentences.

✅ Give at least two effects.
