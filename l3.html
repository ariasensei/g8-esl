Grammar studio

Be Going To for Future Plans

Use be going to when you talk about plans you have already decided.

Positive

Subject + am/is/are + going to + verb

I am going to study. She is going to travel. They are going to play soccer.

Negative

Subject + am/is/are + not + going to + verb

I am not going to stay home. He isn’t going to watch TV.

Questions

Am / Is / Are + subject + going to + verb?

Are you going to cook? Is she going to visit her friend?

Present or future plan?

Today → Present: “I study English.”

Future Plan: “I’m going to study English tomorrow.”

Reading activity

Emma’s Weekend

Read and notice the future plans

Emma has many plans for the weekend. On Saturday morning, she is going to clean her room. In the afternoon, she is going to visit her grandparents. On Sunday, she is going to go shopping with her mother. In the evening, she is going to watch a movie and prepare for school.

My weekend planner

Example paragraph

Next weekend, I am going to visit my cousins. We are going to play games together. I am going to help my aunt cook dinner. We are going to eat pizza and ice cream. On Sunday, I am going to study English and finish my homework. I am excited because I love spending time with my family.
