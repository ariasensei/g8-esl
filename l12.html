3 Timed Reading: The Group Project Problem

Read aloud clearly. Target: about 1 minute 30 seconds–2 minutes. (10 minutes)

The Group Project Problem

On Tuesday, four students were preparing a science presentation. Their project was due on Friday, but they had a problem: two members had completed their parts, while the others had not started. Everyone felt stressed.

“We should divide the remaining work into smaller tasks,” suggested Lina. “That way, we can finish one step at a time.” Daniel agreed, but he was worried about their classmate Noah, who had missed two meetings.

Instead of blaming Noah, the group decided to ask what had happened. Noah explained that his computer had stopped working. “You could use the computers in the library,” said Lina. “If you need help, we can work together after school.” Noah thanked them and promised to finish his slides the next day.

The students also created a shared checklist and chose a time to practise their presentation. By Thursday afternoon, they had finished. Their teacher praised their teamwork, not only their slides.

The group learned an important lesson: when a problem appears, it is better to listen, communicate, and find a practical solution than to blame someone immediately.

4 Grammar: Should, Shouldn't & Could

Choose the right expression for advice. (10 minutes)

should

Recommend a good action.

You should make a study plan.

shouldn't

Advise against a bad idea.

You shouldn't skip breakfast.

could

Offer a possible option.

You could ask your teacher for help.

Grammar rule: subject + should / shouldn't / could + base verb. Say “You should study,” not “You should to study.”

7 Final Review: Flip & Recall

Click a card to reveal its answer. (5 minutes)

What does “advice” mean?

An idea about what someone should do.

What does “should” express?

A recommended action.

When do we use “shouldn’t”?

To advise against an action.

What does “could” express?

A possible option or suggestion.

What verb form follows should?

The base verb: should study, should rest.

What makes advice helpful?

A kind, realistic suggestion with a reason.
