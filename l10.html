Reading Practice

What Might School Look Like in 2035?

Schools are already changing because of new technology. In the future, students may use more digital tools, virtual reality, and artificial intelligence in their classes. However, this does not mean teachers will disappear.

One likely change is more personalized learning. Students may receive activities based on their own level and learning needs. Technology could help teachers quickly see which students need extra practice and which students are ready for more difficult work.

Another possibility is that classrooms will become more flexible. Some lessons may happen face-to-face, while others may happen online. Students might also work with classmates from different countries through video calls and shared digital projects.

Even with these changes, teachers will probably remain important. Technology can provide information, but teachers can guide students, explain difficult ideas, and give encouragement. The future classroom may become more digital, but human communication will still matter.

Grammar Focus: Will, May, and Might

will

Use for a strong prediction.

Technology will continue to change.

may

Use for a possible future event.

Students may use more virtual tools.

might

Use for a weaker possibility.

Some schools might become fully digital.

Easy guide: will = strong prediction • may = possible • might = less certain possibility

Practice

1. In the future, people ______ use more electric cars.

2. Technology ______ continue to develop.

3. Some jobs ______ be replaced by machines, but we are not sure.

Homework

Topic: What Will Life Be Like in 2040?

Write 8–10 sentences.

✅ Give at least three predictions.
✅ Use will at least once.
✅ Use may or might.
✅ Give reasons for your predictions.
✅ Give at least one example.
✅ End with a concluding sentence.

Writing Formula

Prediction → Reason → Example → Another Prediction → Conclusion
